The lifecycle: ten Stages, moved by Signals

The Needs you flag can sit on any Stage. If CI passes and the PR needs no reviewer, the Task skips In review. Loop budgets stop endless loops: 3 CI debug attempts, 3 Responding rounds per review, 2 Worker restarts.

How it sits on Rex

Rex stores no metadata on its objects, so state.db keeps each Task's session, tab, and block IDs. When Rex starts again, the Scheduler compares the two and restarts lost Workers.

The Tasks, titles, and PR numbers are illustrative. The full rules are in docs/spec/mvp.md, GLOSSARY.md, and docs/adr in the lineup repo. The diagrams are not to scale.
